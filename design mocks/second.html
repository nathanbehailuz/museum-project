Containers of Architectural & Ritual Display

Curatorial Rationale

The floral specimen’s relocation from soil to interior space necessitated specialized material engineering. During the seventeenth-century Dutch tulip mania, vessels evolved into monumental, multi-spouted delftware tulipieres designed to sustain fragile severed stems while demonstrating dynastic prestige and mastery over global botanic trade.
